Midway Crusade

A personal project done during my last year at Otis College of Art and Design, built to showcase story, mood, design, and illustration.

Year2023
OrganizationStudent, Otis College of Art and Design
RoleConcept Design
ToolsProcreate, Photoshop

Character Design

Ci, the schoolgirl protagonist; the Spirit, the ship's mysterious soul; and the Bellboy, its only working staff. Each is explored through silhouette, color, and turnaround.

Environment Design

The Bellboy's room, the ship's only lived-in space, explored as a rough perspective sketch, an isometric cutaway, and a hero desk detail rendered across three lighting passes.
