Cambridge Redevelopment Authority

A nonprofit dedicated to neighborhood revitalization and equitable growth in Cambridge, MA, driving urban redevelopment, affordable housing, and public space initiatives. As a Communications and Graphic Design Intern, I worked across print and digital media, balancing brand guidelines with clear, accessible design for diverse audiences.

CRA 2025 Annual Report

Designed the CRA's 69th Annual Report, a 48-page publication covering the organization's 2025 projects, financials, and strategic priorities. Responsible for full layout, typography, infographics, and print production coordination.

Brandbook

Developed the CRA's brand system, including the brandbook, PowerPoint presentation template, and email marketing templates in Constant Contact, establishing consistent visual standards across print and digital communications. Full Brandbook →

Bishop Allen Drive Logo Design

Designed a logo, balancing historic reference and modern identity. The mark incorporates brick texture to honor the building's history while angular forms and a figure-based composition reflect its role as a community space for nonprofits and startups.

Scrim

A large-format construction scrim for the 1175 Cambridge Street redevelopment site, coordinated with the vendor through multiple revision rounds to deliver final production files.

Construction Template

Developed a standardized signage template for future CRA construction projects, ensuring visual consistency with the organization's brand across all upcoming sites.

Cover Report Design

Designed cover variations for the Central Square Demonstration Project Plan, a digital report produced by the Cambridge Redevelopment Authority.

Flyers

Flyers designed across multiple CRA initiatives and timeframes, covering community events, program announcements, and public outreach. Each was built for both digital distribution (social, email, web) and physical print, adapted to CRA's brand guidelines across formats.

Tablecloth Design

Designed multiple variations for an event tablecloth, managing the full production process from concept to completion, including vendor outreach, pricing, sample review, and final print coordination.

RLF Squarespace Page

Created custom header illustrations visualizing the cyclical nature of the loan system, built within Squarespace platform constraints ahead of the program's public launch. The site is not currently public, as it wasn't finished by the time my internship ended. I designed both this page and its underlying information architecture, not just the header illustrations.

Galaxy Park Sticker

Illustrated and designed a die-cut sticker featuring the native plant species found at Galaxy Park, produced for community distribution.
