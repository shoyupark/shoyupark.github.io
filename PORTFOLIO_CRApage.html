Brand & Communications · 2025–2026

Cambridge Redevelopment Authority

A nonprofit dedicated to neighborhood revitalization and equitable growth in Cambridge, MA. As a Communications and Graphic Design Intern, I worked across print, digital, and environmental design — balancing brand standards with materials that had to communicate clearly to diverse public audiences.

The scope spanned the organization's full communications output: a flagship annual report, a complete brand system refresh, environmental signage, digital content, community illustrations, and event materials. The design incorporates brick texture referencing the building's industrial past, while angular forms and a figure-based composition reflect its current role as a community space for nonprofits and startups.
