Hikari Ferramentas

Hikari Ferramentas is a Brazilian distribution company specializing in professional tools and measurement equipment.

As Motion and Graphic Designer, I collaborated with the marketing team across product and marketing deliverables, from retail packaging and product photography to social media content and video production, translating technical product information into clear, compelling design for retail and digital channels.
