Rogue Defense

Designed a series of graphic tees for Rogue Defense, an LA-based streetwear brand for low-key firearm enthusiasts. Designed for DTG and silk screen.

Year2024
OrganizationFreelance for JSB
RoleApparel Graphic Design, T-Shirt Design, Mockup Design

Graphic Tee Design

Rogue Defense wanted its gun-culture graphics to read as young and casual first, not the typical gun-enthusiast aesthetic. The challenge was tone: keeping every design clever and satirical enough that you'd wear it without immediately clocking it as a gun brand.
